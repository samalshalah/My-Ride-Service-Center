How Worn Suspension Affects Safety — Not Just Comfort

Your suspension system keeps your tires in contact with the road and allows you to maintain control during emergency maneuvers. When components wear out, the consequences range from uneven tire wear and a rough ride to serious loss of vehicle control. At My Ride Service Center, we perform thorough suspension inspections — lifting the vehicle, shaking each wheel, and testing every component before recommending any repair.

Common Suspension Problems We Diagnose and Fix

Clunking or knocking sounds over bumps (typically ball joints or sway bar links), excessive body roll in corners (worn shocks or struts), pulling to one side while driving (alignment or tie rod issues), and vibration at highway speeds (wheel bearing or alignment problems) are the most common complaints we resolve. We identify the exact cause before touching a single part.

4-Wheel Computerized Alignment After Every Suspension Repair

After every suspension repair, we perform a 4-wheel computerized alignment to ensure your tires are pointed correctly. Misalignment after suspension work causes rapid, uneven tire wear and handling issues — an alignment is not optional after replacing control arms, tie rods, or struts. Proper alignment also improves fuel economy and extends tire life by thousands of miles.

Our Suspension & Steering Repairs Process

1

Suspension Inspection

We lift the vehicle and inspect all four corners — shaking wheels, checking for play in joints and bearings, and measuring shock/strut condition.

2

Steering System Check

We inspect tie rods, steering rack boots, power steering hoses, and rack-and-pinion for wear and leaks.

3

Component Replacement

We replace worn or damaged components using quality parts that meet or exceed OEM specifications and torque all hardware to specification.

4

4-Wheel Alignment

We perform a computerized 4-wheel alignment after any suspension work to set camber, caster, toe, and thrust angle to factory specifications.

5

Road Test

We test drive the vehicle on varying road surfaces to confirm ride, steering feel, and handling have been fully restored.
